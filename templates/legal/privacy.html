PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

EduClip turns YouTube links you submit into study material. This page states plainly what we store and why.

What we store

Submitted video URLs and identifiers, generated chapters, summaries, keywords, flashcards, and aggregate analytics — persisted in MongoDB Atlas to serve your dashboard and skip reprocessing repeat submissions.

What we don't store

There are no user accounts, so there are no passwords, emails, or profiles. We never sell data or use it for advertising.

Third parties

Transcripts come from YouTube, AI analysis from your configured LLM provider (OpenAI or Google), and playback embeds the YouTube player — each governed by its own policy.

Deletion

Deleting a video removes its record, flashcards, analytics, and cached transcript segments. Open an issue on GitHub for anything else.
